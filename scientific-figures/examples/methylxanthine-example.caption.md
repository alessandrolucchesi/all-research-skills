**Methylxanthine exposure and the osteopenic phenotype.** Methylxanthine exposure lowers bone mineral density through three converging routes, adenosine receptor antagonism, a cAMP-driven shift of the osteoblast RANKL to osteoprotegerin ratio, and negative calcium balance; the convergence is proposed, each link is cited.

Proposed and not tested (dotted, unfilled head): Bone resorption to Bone mineral density.

Cited for the established steps: Barger-Lux 1995, Boyle 2003, Essayan 2001, Fredholm 1999, Fu 2002, Ma 2001, Massey 1993, Mediero 2013.
