**Tirzepatide in obesity, glycaemic control and adipose inflammation.** Dual GIP and GLP-1 receptor agonism improves glycaemia through the islet and through adipose lipid handling, while the fall in adipose inflammation follows the loss of fat mass rather than a demonstrated direct action on the macrophage.

Shown as association without demonstrated causality (long dash): Tirzepatide to TNF and IL-6.

Cited for the established steps: Coskun 2018, Frias 2021, Jastreboff 2022, Ouchi 2011, Ridker 2003, Samms 2020, Samms 2021, Weisberg 2003, Wilson 2022.
